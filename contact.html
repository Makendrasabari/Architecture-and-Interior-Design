Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding our commission process, architectural licensing, and project management.

How do I initiate a new project commission with STACKLY?
You can submit our confidential inquiry form above or contact our Salem Atelier directly at salem@stackly-studio.com. Our principals review all proposals within 24 hours to schedule an initial consultation.
What geographic regions does STACKLY service?
While our headquarters and primary design lab are located in Salem, Massachusetts, STACKLY leads private residential and commercial commissions across North America, Europe, and Asia.
How does STACKLY manage project timelines and construction budgets?
We integrate building information modeling (BIM) with real-time contractor tendering and weekly milestone audits. Our project management team maintains militant oversight over general contractors to prevent cost overruns and delays.
Can I schedule a private visit to the Salem Atelier?
Yes, our Salem studio at 142 Washington Street welcomes prospective clients for private consultations, material gallery tours, and virtual reality walkthroughs Monday through Saturday by appointment.
Does STACKLY provide turnkey interior design alongside architectural services?
Yes, we specialize in unified architectural and interior creation. Our interior team handles custom joinery, rare stone sourcing, lighting control design, and full FF&E procurement to deliver a harmonized space.
